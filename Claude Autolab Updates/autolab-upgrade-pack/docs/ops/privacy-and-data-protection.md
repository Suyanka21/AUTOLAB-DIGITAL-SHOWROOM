# Privacy & Data Protection Position (draft for GATE-10)

**This is a checklist, not legal advice.** The lead form collects names and phone numbers, so Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 applies. Confirm every point below with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) or a lawyer.

## 1. Roles
- **AutoLab** is the data controller (it decides why the data is collected).
- The developer and the hosting and database providers act as processors.
- Whoever holds the data must be able to say where it is stored.

## 2. Registration
Organisations processing personal data may need to register with the ODPC unless an exemption applies. Reported exemption criteria depend on turnover and staff numbers, with exceptions by sector. Verify AutoLab's status with the ODPC and record it here: ______.

## 3. What the site must have before launch
- [ ] A privacy notice page: who AutoLab is, what is collected (name, phone, optional email, the configuration), why, who sees it, how long it is kept, how to ask for access or deletion.
- [ ] Consent wording on the enquiry form that matches the notice (the form already has a consent checkbox).
- [ ] A retention period chosen by AutoLab (for example delete or anonymise enquiries after N months): ______.
- [ ] A way to find and delete one person's data by reference code or phone number (admin route).
- [ ] A breach procedure naming who decides and who notifies. Verify the notification deadline in the Act (reported as 72 hours).
- [ ] Data minimisation: email stays optional; collect nothing the sales team does not need.

## 4. Cross-border storage
If the database region is outside Kenya, personal data leaves the country. Ask the lawyer or the ODPC what safeguards or notices apply, and choose the region with that answer in hand.

## 5. Analytics
Events must not carry names or phone numbers. If cookies or similar identifiers are used, the notice must say so.
