Verification Methodology and Source Selection
This reference package establishes the definitive technical and visual ground truths for the Phase 3 digital asset feasibility spike conducted by AutoLab Digital Showroom. The scope of this asset package is strictly restricted to two primary interior elements of the 2021-generation Mercedes-Benz S-Class Long Wheelbase (V223 platform, pre-facelift interior layout):

GEO_Seat_Front_Driver_Flutes (Driver's front seat upholstery, longitudinal fluting, contour geometry, and seam construction).   

GEO_Dashboard_Screen_Central_OLED (Cascading portrait 12.8-inch OLED display, mounting inclination, bezel framing, and immediate console trim boundaries).   

To preserve absolute geometric fidelity for subsequent Blender polygonal modeling and real-time viewport evaluation, source documents have been processed through a strict hierarchy of visual and technical verification. Primary weight is assigned to official Mercedes-Benz workshop manuals, engineering releases, emergency response structural sheets, and high-resolution manufacturer media kits. Secondary validation is drawn from verified automotive press teardowns and photogrammetric review imagery. Third-party aftermarket measurements and unverified renders have been explicitly excluded to avoid propagating spatial inaccuracies.

DRIVER_SEAT_GEOMETRY.md
Anatomical Architecture and Upholstery Classification
The driver's front seat assembly in the 2021-generation Mercedes-Benz S-Class V223 utilizes a complex, multi-zone ergonomic frame engineered for active pneumatic contouring and thermal management. The visual identity of the seat surface is dictated by the chosen leather upholstery tier. In standard leather and standard Nappa leather specifications, the central backrest and seat cushion inserts feature parallel, vertical longitudinal fluting (piping). This longitudinal fluted configuration is the exact target for the GEO_Seat_Front_Driver_Flutes asset. It must be distinguished from the Exclusive Nappa leather option, which replaces longitudinal fluting with a diamond-quilted stitch lattice across the central panels.   

The overall seat volume is divided into four distinct geometric zones that must be modeled as separate topological sub-assemblies:

Seat Base and Cushion Assembly: The horizontal support structure comprises a central fluted cushion panel, deeply contoured side bolsters angled outward to anchor the driver's thighs, and an adjustable thigh extension pad located along the leading edge. The thigh extension utilizes a gap-free leather bellows mechanism that extends forward by up to 50 mm without revealing internal framework.   

Backrest Assembly: The vertical backrest transitions from a narrowed lower lumbar support region into a broad thoracic shoulder wing zone. The lateral side bolsters project forward at approximately 30° relative to the main backrest plane, housing active pneumatic bladders that inflate during dynamic cornering maneuvers.   

Head Restraint Assembly: The headrest comprises a rigid, height-adjustable core mounted to the seat backrest via twin polished stainless steel stalks. Attached to the front face of this core is the supplementary "Luxury Neck Cushion"—a soft, down-filled pillow secured around the headrest stalk collar via a tailored leather strap and press-stud fastener.   

Lower Cladding and Rail Infrastructure: The lower perimeter of the seat base is encased in matte, injection-molded plastic cladding. This cladding houses seat frame attachment points, track slides, and manual emergency releases, while the primary electrical seat control switches are mounted separately on the door panel module.   

Longitudinal Flute Layout and Seam Geometry
The defining visual characteristic of GEO_Seat_Front_Driver_Flutes is the parallel vertical piping running continuously along the central insert panels. This layout adheres to strict geometric proportions:   

Flute Count and Distribution: The central backrest panel features exactly 8 vertical flutes. These flutes are mirrored on the horizontal cushion panel directly below, maintaining line-of-sight continuity along the longitudinal axis of the vehicle.   

Dimensional Width: The nominal width of each central flute is 
42 mm±1.5 mm across the primary contact zones. To accommodate the natural taper of the backrest frame near the lumbar junction, the outer flutes gradually narrow to 
38 mm at their base before merging into the lower horizontal anchor seam.   

Channel Depth and Cross-Section: Each flute is formed by pulling upholstery leather down into underlying high-density foam troughs via blind tuck-in stitching. This creates a recessed channel depth of 
6.0 mm relative to the crest of the flute padding. The transverse cross-section of each flute exhibits a soft, convex parabolic curve with a crown radius of approximately 
25 mm.   

Seam Construction: Double-stitched French seams run along the structural boundary separating the central fluted inlay from the smooth leather side bolsters. The stitch pitch along these load-bearing seams is uniform at 
3.5 mm, utilizing heavy-duty nylon thread with a subtle satin luster.

Modeling Guidelines for Blender Execution
To achieve render-ready fidelity without generating unmanageable vertex counts, the modeling workflow must combine structured quad topology with targeted displacement mapping:

Topology Hierarchy: Model the backrest, cushion, thigh extension, and headrest as discrete polygonal meshes. Base topology must be strictly quad-dominant, aligning edge loops directly with the 8 longitudinal flute channels to allow clean geometric deformation.

Geometric vs. Baked Flutes: Because the 
6 mm channel recess significantly alters the silhouette of the seat in three-quarter viewing angles, flutes must be modeled directly into the base geometry rather than relying solely on normal maps. Edge creasing or tight support loops should be applied around the channel troughs to maintain sharp seam definition under a Subdivision Surface modifier (Level 2 viewport, Level 3 render).

Thigh Extension Bellows: Ensure the gap-free sliding pad mesh extends under the main seat cushion mesh with a 
1.0 mm shadow gap, preventing surface intersection during potential adjustment animations.   

Wrinkle and Tension Sculpting: Micro-wrinkles resulting from leather tension near seam intersections should be sculpted on a high-density multiresolution layer and baked to a tangent-space normal map (
4096×4096 resolution).

CENTRAL_OLED_GEOMETRY.md
Hardware Integration and Waterfall Console Concept
The centerpiece of the 2021-generation S-Class V223 cockpit is the cascading central multimedia display. Positioned along the vehicle's centerline, the display unit emerges from beneath the central dashboard air vents and flows down toward the center armrest in a layout described by Mercedes-Benz interior designers as a "waterfall" console.   

The physical assembly comprises a monolithic sheet of cold-formed glass mounted over a high-contrast Organic Light-Emitting Diode (OLED) panel. Unlike conventional flat-mounted touchscreens, the V223 central display appears to float above the underlying dashboard trim, supported by a hidden structural neck and framed by an anodized metallic bezel.

Photogrammetric and Perspective Distortion Risks
Wide-Angle Interior Press Photography Distortion:

Manufacturer promotional photography frequently uses ultra-wide lenses (
16 mm to 
20 mm focal length equivalent) to capture expansive cabin shots. This introduces severe barrel distortion, causing the top edge of the central OLED screen to appear wider than its base and artificially exaggerating seat bolster flare.   

Correction Protocol: Modeling agents must not calibrate camera focal length directly from interior press shots. All camera match setups in Blender must use a standardized 
50 mm perspective lens context, calibrating viewport scale exclusively against derived millimeter specs (
219.5 mm screen width, 
530 mm seat cushion width).

Model Year Component Confusion (2026+ Facelift Horizon):

Updated 2026/2027 S-Class media introduces the MBUX Superscreen option, embedding a single continuous glass panel across the entire passenger dashboard.   

Correction Protocol: Modeling agents must strictly reject any reference photography displaying a continuous glass dashboard spanning toward the passenger door. Reference alignment must adhere exclusively to the standalone floating 12.8-inch portrait display layout characteristic of the 2021–2025 V223 generation.
