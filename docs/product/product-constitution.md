## 1. The North Star
> ### **"Visualise the transformation before you commit to it."**
> That is the fundamental promise and commercial rationale of the AutoLab Digital Showroom.

---

## 2. Product Mission & Vision

### Mission
Give AutoLab customers a way to experience, configure, and visualise their proposed interior transformation before the physical work begins in the workshop.

The product should make the customer feel actively involved in co-creating their interior rather than simply purchasing an upholstery service or receiving an arbitrary price quote.

### Vision
The AutoLab Digital Showroom must feel less like an ordinary transactional website and more like stepping into a digital sanctuary of AutoLab's bespoke design atelier:
```
[ Enter Showroom ] ──▶ [ Select Vehicle ] ──▶ [ Configure Interior ] ──▶ [ Visualise 3D Transformation ] ──▶ [ Review Design Summary ] ──▶ [ Engage AutoLab Atelier ]
```
The experience must communicate the identical values that define AutoLab's physical craftsmanship:
- **Precision:** Millimeter-accurate proportions, flawless stitch geometry, and tight panel shut lines.
- **Luxury:** Sophisticated materials (Nappa leather, open-pore timber, forged carbon, ambient fiber optics).
- **Craftsmanship:** Tactile textures, authentic grain, and bespoke contrast stitching.
- **Transformation:** The emotional "WOW" of seeing a factory or tired cabin reborn into an bespoke masterpiece.
- **Confidence:** Absolute clarity on materials, tones, and visual composition prior to physical disassembly.

---

## 3. The Business Problem It Solves

AutoLab's customers recognize the desire to transform their vehicle interior, but struggle to imagine the finished aesthetic before the physical work begins. Because AutoLab's work is deeply bespoke, decisions regarding:
- Leather grade and primary hide tone;
- Secondary bolster accents and two-tone splits;
- Accent thread color and quilting geometry (diamond, french seam, double-lap);
- Dashboard and door veneer finishes (open-pore wood vs. forged carbon);
- Active ambient lighting harmony,

historically required multiple physical visits to the showroom or lengthy WhatsApp discussions with swatches. The Digital Showroom enables clients to **participate in the design journey remotely and immediately experience the visual payoff**, honoring AutoLab's guiding creed:
> *"AutoLab should offer an experience rather than feeling like a transaction."*

## 4. Product Category Definition

### What AutoLab Digital Showroom IS:
- **A bespoke interactive automotive interior visualisation and configuration experience.**
- A high-touch sales-enablement tool designed to elevate qualified lead conversations.
- A digital showcase of AutoLab's craftsmanship and personalization capabilities.

### What AutoLab Digital Showroom IS NOT:
- **NOT an automotive marketplace or classifieds platform.**
- **NOT an e-commerce platform with automated checkout, shopping carts, or online payment gateways.**
- **NOT a generic AI car-design sandbox or toy generator.**
- **NOT a CRM, workshop management system, ERP, or inventory tracker.**
- **NOT an automated instant-quotation calculator.** (Quotations depend on vehicle condition, mileage, seat structural wear, and custom client requests evaluated physically).
- **NOT a complete vehicle exterior configurator.** (V1 focus is strictly the interior cabin).

---

## 5. The Product Constitution

### We Will:
1. **Prioritise the customer's ability to visualise transformation** above arbitrary feature bloat.
2. **Represent AutoLab's real physical capabilities** and approved catalog options only.
3. **Make personalisation central to the experience** (Material ➔ Colour ➔ Accent Thread ➔ Visual Composition).
4. **Prioritise premium luxury interiors** where transformation impact is highest.
5. **Preserve the physical showroom and personal consultation** as the vital next step in the sales journey.
6. **Architect around a vehicle-asset abstraction** so future vehicles (Range Rover, Toyota Hilux) can enter the pipeline seamlessly.
7. **Keep the initial product strictly focused on V1 scope.**
8. **Use technology and AI to amplify human craftsmanship, not to replace it.**

### We Will NOT:
1. **Build a generic automotive platform** with global VIN decoders, generic databases, or unrelated car models.
2. **Turn AutoLab into an e-commerce business** with buy buttons, shopping carts, or fixed automated pricing.
3. **Attempt to replace the physical showroom** or consultative customer relationship.
4. **Promise configurations, materials, or patterns that AutoLab cannot physically source or fabricate.**
5. **Allow AI-generated concepts to redefine AutoLab's business identity without client approval.**
6. **Add features merely because they are technically feasible.**
7. **Expand the scope without an explicit commercial decision and separate quotation.**
